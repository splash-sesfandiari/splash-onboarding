Common questions

Is Splash free to use as a creator?

Yes — there's no upfront cost to become a creator partner. You earn a cut of every contest you run. Splash handles the platform, payments, and compliance.

How exactly do I earn money?

You earn a revenue share on every contest you run — not just when you bring in new users. Run a weekly Pick'Em for your audience? You earn on that. Run a season-long Survivor? You earn on that too. The more contests you run, the more you earn.

Do I need a large following to apply?

Not necessarily. We look at engagement and community quality, not just follower count. Some of our most successful creator partners started with audiences under 10K. If your audience is passionate about sports, we want to talk.

What game formats can I run?

You have access to five formats: Survivor, Pick'Em, Tiers, One & Done, and Quick Picks — across NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, PGA, NCAAF, and NCAAB. You choose which formats fit your content style and audience best.

How long does the application review take?

We review every application personally and typically respond within a few business days. If we think you're a fit, we'll set up a quick call to walk you through onboarding.

Are the contests peer-to-peer?

Always. Your fans play against each other — never against the house. This is a core part of how Splash works, and it's what makes the community dynamics so strong.
