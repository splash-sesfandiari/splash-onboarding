Five formats. Endless ways to play.

Mix and match contest formats to keep your audience engaged across every sport and every week of the season.

Most Popular

Survivor

Survive and advance. Pick a winner each week — one loss and you're out. Creates massive engagement through an entire season.

Pick'Em

Pick a group of winners each week and earn points for correct calls. Great for weekly podcast drops and consistent content rhythms.

Tiers

Draft 26 players across tiers, match up against real opponents, and rack up points. Win big even if you miss one pick.

One & Done

Survivor meets Tiers. Draft your lineup, but once you use a player, they're gone for the rest of the contest. Strategic depth your audience will love.

Quick Picks

Draft your dream squad each slate. Earn points based on in-game performance. Perfect for daily and weekly cadences.
